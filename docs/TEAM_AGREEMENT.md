## 1. Purpose
This Agreement establishes the ownership, intellectual-property, confidentiality, contribution, commercialisation, security, and decision-making rules for the Agro-Shield team as the project moves from development/hackathon work toward a production commercial platform.

## 2. What Agro-Shield Includes
Where owned or created by the team, Agro-Shield includes:
- source code and application code;
- backend, frontend, APIs and integrations;
- database schemas and migrations;
- system/software architecture;
- UI/UX designs and components;
- documentation and specifications;
- project-specific AI prompts, workflows and logic;
- marketplace, ordering, payment, commission and settlement workflows;
- branding, names, logos, slogans and marketing materials;
- product roadmaps and unreleased features; and
- project-specific research and documentation.

Third-party materials remain subject to their own licenses.

## 5. Intellectual Property
Subject to applicable law and the ownership structure, each contributor agrees that original work created specifically for Agro-Shield as part of their assigned contribution is intended to belong to the agreed Agro-Shield owner/entity. Where a formal IP assignment is required, the contributor agrees to execute the necessary documents.

## 6. Pre-Existing Work
A contributor retains ownership of IP created independently before joining Agro-Shield unless separately transferred in writing. Important pre-existing code, designs, libraries or inventions intended for incorporation must be disclosed and appropriately licensed before use.

## 7. Contributions
Contributors must perform assigned responsibilities, communicate material changes, use version control, avoid malicious code, protect credentials, document important production changes, respect code review/security procedures, and comply with third-party licenses.

A contribution to the repository does not automatically give a contributor ownership of the entire Agro-Shield platform.

## 8. Repository and Source-Code Restrictions
The proprietary Agro-Shield repository and source code must not be published, sold, licensed, transferred, given to outsiders, or used to create a competing commercial product without authorization. Normal collaboration through the official repository is permitted.

## 9. Commercialisation
No individual contributor may independently sell, license, franchise, or commercialise Agro-Shield as though they were its sole owner. Major commercial decisions must follow the team's agreed governance and ownership structure.

## 10. Revenue and Compensation
Revenue generated by Agro-Shield belongs to the agreed legal owner/entity and is distributed according to a separately documented arrangement.

**Revenue-sharing arrangement:** [Insert arrangement]  
**Equity/founder arrangement:** [Insert arrangement]

Examples of revenue include marketplace commissions, subscriptions, service fees, enterprise contracts, licensing, grants and investment proceeds.

## 11. Marketplace and Payment Funds
Customer payments, seller funds, commissions, refunds and settlement funds must be handled through approved business/payment systems. No team member may divert such funds to a personal account except where expressly authorised as part of an approved process.

## 12. Confidentiality
Confidential information includes private source code, database credentials, API keys, payment credentials, customer/farmer information, business plans, pricing, security configurations, partner information, unreleased features and financial information. Confidential information must not be disclosed to unauthorised persons. This obligation continues after leaving the project, subject to applicable law.

## 13. Security
Production credentials must not be committed to the repository. Contributors must use approved secret-management methods, report compromised credentials immediately, avoid unauthorized production access, and privately report security vulnerabilities.

## 14. User Data
Customer, farmer, buyer, seller, payment and other user data must be handled only for legitimate Agro-Shield purposes and in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements. Production data must not be copied to personal devices or external systems without authorization.

## 15. Open-Source Dependencies
Agro-Shield may use open-source libraries and third-party services. Contributors must respect their licenses and must not remove required copyright or license notices.

## 16. Branding
The Agro-Shield name, logo, slogan, visual identity and project-specific branding are project assets to the extent owned by the project. No contributor may independently register, sell, transfer or commercially exploit the Agro-Shield brand without authorization.

## 17. Hackathon Materials
Where Agro-Shield was developed or submitted for a hackathon, the team must comply with the organizer's official rules concerning submitted code, IP, licensing, publicity and prizes. This Agreement does not override those rules.

## 18. Leaving the Team
Leaving the project does not grant a departing contributor permission to copy proprietary source code, publish confidential materials, sell Agro-Shield, claim sole ownership, or use confidential information for an unauthorized competing purpose. Confidential project materials should be returned or securely deleted as reasonably required.

## 19. Removal
Project access may be suspended or removed for serious misconduct, unauthorized disclosure, misuse of funds, unauthorized production access, intentional security compromise, fraud, deliberate destruction of project assets, or repeated serious violations.

## 22. Amendments
