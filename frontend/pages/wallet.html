How transfers work

  1. Open your bank app and choose a transfer to another bank.
  2. Enter the account number shown here and confirm the name.
  3. Send any amount in Naira.
  4. Your wallet is credited only after Flutterwave confirms the transfer.

🔒 Your BVN or NIN is used only to open the account with Flutterwave. It is not stored by Agro-Shield and never appears in your wallet history.

If a transfer stays pending, do not send it again. Check your deposit history first.
